1. CORE USER IDEOLOGY & PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY (DO NOT ALTER OR FORGET)
================================================================================
• "Knowing concepts and all is great, but it all relies on your ability to arrive at the final answer. Write like this."
• 100% PURE mental speed calculation drills. Zero word problems, zero QA conceptual geometry/algebra fluff.
• "Focus on those which are commonly tested, not edge cases." (e.g. 7/19 or 8/17 are obscure edge cases that are excluded).
• "In the real CAT exam, 90% of /12 and /15 questions are clustered right around 50%... So prefer those. Run this logic across all topics."
• "The primary bottleneck is not working memory overload—it is arriving at the final numerical answer accurately and rapidly under time pressure."
• Daily cognitive conditioning: A tool built to be used daily (e.g. 100 days) to permanently hardwire number sense and instant recall.

3. QUESTION GENERATION ENGINE RULES & SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================
1. "Anchors of the Sprint" Protocol:
   - Initialized at sprint start via `initSessionAnchors()`.
   - Silently picks 1 High-Yield Square (17-32), 1 High-Yield Table (14-29), and 1 High-Yield Fraction (5/12, 7/12, 7/15, 8/15, 3/8, 5/8, 7/8, 2/7, 5/7, etc.).
   - Weaved into the sprint 3 to 4 times across distinct algebraic templates:
     • (sq² + offset), (sq² - offset), (offset - sq²), ((sq² × M) + offset).
     • (T × M + offset), (T × M - offset), (Dividend ÷ T + offset).
     • (Frac% of Base + offset), (Frac% of Base - offset), (Frac% of Base + Table).

2. Dual-Layer Anti-Repetition Cache:
   - Level 1: 35-question signature buffer (`recentSignatures`) prevents identical questions in a sprint.
   - Level 2: 6-family buffer (`recentFamilies`) prevents back-to-back testing of the same base element while allowing anchor rotation every 5-7 questions.

3. High-Yield Topic Pools:
   - Powers: Squares (12² to 30² plus 31², 32², 33² with (a+b)² expansion hints: (30+d)² = 900 + 60d + d²), Cubes (7³ to 18³), Base-2 (2¹–2¹²), Base-3 (3¹–3⁷), Base-4 (4¹–4⁶), Base-5 (5¹–5⁶), Base-6 (6¹–6⁵), Base-7 (7¹–7⁴), Difference of Squares (a² - b²), Base 50/100 Near Squaring.
   - Fractions & %: Denominators 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20. Percentage reversals (250, 750, 125, 375, 625), km/h factor drills (5/18 and 18/5).
   - Factorials: High-yield ratios (8!/6!, 7!/5!, 6!/3!) multiplied by scalars and factored with offsets.
   - Tables & Products: Heavy emphasis on mental prime multiplication and prime division (3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23, 29) coupled with multi-term operations (never in isolation, never back-to-back), CAT Special Numbers (1001 = 7×11×13, 10001 = 73×137, 111 = 3×37, 10101 = 111×91, 999 = 27×37) with prime factorization hints, Tables 14–29, and Twin Products (x-d)(x+d) around midpoints (25, 30, 35, 40, 50, 60).
   - Addition & Subtraction: High-frequency weighting (~30% of pool when active); dedicated 2-Digit versions (2-term speed pair, 3-term mixed, 4–5 term chain, DILR column sum), dedicated 3-Digit versions (2-term speed pair, 3-term mixed, 4-term multi-term, DILR column sum), mixed 2D+3D adjustments, and base complementary subtractions (500, 1000, 1500, 2000).
   - Roots & Decimals: Square roots (14²-36²), Cube roots (7³-19³), Decimal Multipliers (0.375, 0.625, 0.875, 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 0.833, 1.333, 1.667).
   - Hybrids: Multi-step cross-topic questions (Powers + Tables, Fractions + Tables, Factorials + Fractions).

4. Scoring & Feedback:
   - +3 for Correct, -1 for Wrong, 0 for Unattempted/Skipped.
   - Atomic `isTransitioning` lock prevents double-submission or double-skipping during the 280ms feedback animation.

5. Dynamic Ideal Pacing:
   - Unique target seconds evaluated per question based on operator composition via `computeDynamicIdealTime()`.
